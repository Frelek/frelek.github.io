# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers at aerospace companies (launch vehicles, engines, entry systems, CFD teams) who open the site from a CV, LinkedIn or an application, usually on a laptop, and decide in a minute or two whether Kacper is worth interviewing. A smaller group, mostly fellow engineers, reads the project pages and tries the calculators to judge depth.

## Product Purpose
A bilingual (EN default, PL) personal CV site for Kacper Frelek, M.Sc. Eng. in aerospace engineering (Warsaw University of Technology, 2026). Success: a visitor quickly understands his role, education and strongest work, trusts the claims, and downloads the CV or gets in touch.

## Positioning
Real, checkable engineering work: his own CFD results, meshes, plots and a master's thesis with a 16-case validation matrix against Caltech T5 data, plus working calculators. Nothing on the site may claim more than the source documents support.

## Operating Context
Static site on GitHub Pages (custom domain frelekkacper.com). Single HTML page per language with hash routing between sections; KaTeX for equations; a lightbox for figures. English page `index.html`, Polish page `index_pl.html`, shared `styles.css` and `script.js`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections: overview/CV, EDC aeromechanics role, impinging jet (Framatome), NACA 2412 turbulence comparison, Starship re-entry aerodynamics, Falcon 9 grid fins (B.Sc.), MSL master's thesis, urban wind, Aero Lab (y+ and Fay-Riddell calculators), travel.
- First visit plays a short rocket-launch intro once per browser; skippable; off under reduced motion.
- Rocket Factory Augsburg: an aerodynamics internship, kept short and neutral (user-confirmed); dates and details are undecided.
- EDC diagrams (Campbell, Goodman, mode shapes, LCF/HCF) are generic illustrations, not project data (user-confirmed), and must be labelled as such.
- Master's degree is complete (user-confirmed).

## Brand Commitments
Professional CV tone, plain first-person English, no hype. User chose a light, clean editorial look with one restrained accent where his real CFD images carry the page.

## Product Principles
1. Every number and claim traces to a document; when unsure, say less.
2. Show the work (figures, tables, data) instead of describing it with adjectives.
3. Honest results, including what did not match, read as more senior than inflated ones.
4. Fast to scan for a recruiter, deep enough for an engineer.
