# Surface: index.html / index_pl.html (personal CV site)

Mode: Experience (portfolio). Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience and job: aerospace recruiters and engineers deciding whether to interview Kacper; primary action is opening the CV PDF or emailing him.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site reads like a well-made engineering technical report: figures with numbered captions, real tables, and equations set properly, so the work itself is the design. It refuses the portfolio-template hero with gradient text, stat counters, glass cards and emoji.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper (#f6f7f5), near-black blue-grey ink (#14181c), hairline rules in ink at low opacity, one accent: International Orange, #c43c0a where it carries text and #e8490f where it is decorative, used only for link hover, primary-button hover, the rocket flame and figure numbers. Active navigation is marked in ink. Archivo for headings and UI, Source Serif 4 for body text, tabular figures for data. Figures sit full-bleed in their column with caption text set small under a short rule.

STORY: In seconds the visitor knows: aerospace engineer, CFD and aerothermodynamics, Warsaw; currently aeromechanics at EDC; master's thesis on MSL aeroheating. Then they scan experience and education, open one or two projects, and download the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: name large, one-line role, three-sentence intro, actions (Download CV primary, Email, LinkedIn). Right: portrait. Below, "Figure 1" (thesis heat-flux distribution, 2T second order) with a marginal caption under an ink rule.

FORM: technical-report CV, seed: none (pinned), "Light, clean editorial (Recommended): Paper-white page, dark ink text, one restrained accent color, real CFD images doing the work." and the user's own brief for the first surface: "when someone enters my page first time, there is a rocket at first, it launches leaving smoke and fire behind. Then it disappears into my page." Signature interaction: first-visit rocket launch (canvas fire and smoke), then the smoke clears into the page.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
